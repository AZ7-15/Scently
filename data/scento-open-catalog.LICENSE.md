The bundled JSON contains 1479 original published records. Scently converts JSONL to JSON and adapts field names, note stages, concentration labels and season names for display. The publisher's `popularity_rank` is retained as its best-known ranking signal; it is not a sales count. Supplier metadata is credited and is not independently verified by this conversion. Unknown values remain unknown. Product and ingredient images are not part of this data license; their sources are credited separately.

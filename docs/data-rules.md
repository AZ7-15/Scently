# Evidence and ranking rules

- Ratings average published reviews. Hidden/pending reviews do not enter public aggregates.
- Longevity is median hours; projection is the most frequent category, with first-reported order resolving ties. Fewer than 20 reviews are labeled early impressions.
- Season counts are review mentions. Editorial seasons and accord order are opinions, never invented vote percentages.
- Note stages follow the source. Missing classifications are not inferred.
- Current offers must be in stock, correct for the selected region/currency, not future-dated, and verified within 48 hours. Comparable variants share volume, concentration, condition and packaging.
- Complete totals rank ahead of incomplete subtotals. Unknown charges are disclosed. Hard budgets require known shipping and tax.
- Finder weights: notes 30; longevity 20; projection 20; season 10; budget 10; style 10; an occasion request adds 10. Only requested dimensions enter the denominator. Exclusions remove candidates. Every unmet preference is a gap; any gap makes a result a near match. No pseudo-scientific match percentages appear.
- Models interpret preferences but cannot author product facts. Unknown requested notes cannot create products.
- Never import fabricated reviews, measurements, prices or purchase-verification badges. Fictional records must be explicitly labeled demo data.
